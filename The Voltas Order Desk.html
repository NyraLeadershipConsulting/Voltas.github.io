How it runs
  1. Teams of 4 to 5. About 5 minutes to build the story.
  2. Drag cards onto the story line. On a phone or tablet, tap a card and then tap a slot.
  3. Submit your order. Then 60 seconds to say it out loud.

Follow-up prompts
  1. Why did you put this card first?Listen for: you need to know what is happening before you hear there is a problem.
  2. Why did that card come next?Listen for: that is where it goes wrong.
  3. Why does the question sit there?Listen for: once the problem is clear, everyone wants to know what happens now.
  4. What do the last four cards do?Listen for: they explain, then say what to do.

The next step: lead with the answer

SCQA is how you work the story out. Once you know the answer, you can lead with it. Say what the listener needs first, then the reasons, then only the evidence that backs them up.
